## What's new in 4.5.7

### Mouse and keyboard

- **With a capture card, PushFPS now tells you what's missing.** A capture card only brings the picture and
  the sound in, so your keys reach the PS5 through Remote Play. When mouse and keyboard is on and nothing can
  send them to the console yet, the *Mouse and keyboard* row says why and offers the button that fixes it:
  turn on *Control the PS5 through Remote Play*, pair your PS5 first, reconnect, or enter the PS5's sign-in
  PIN. Before, the switch was on and simply nothing happened.
- **Clicking the picture or pressing F12 says why** when mouse and keyboard can't start yet, instead of doing
  nothing.
- **A notice tells you when it's ready**: once a PS5 stream, an Xbox stream or PS5 controller control can
  take your mouse and keyboard, PushFPS says to click the picture or press F12.
- **Shooting no longer ends it by accident.** While the console has your mouse, the hidden cursor now stays
  in the middle of the picture. Before, it could drift onto the playback bar at the bottom of the window, and
  a left click there took the mouse and keyboard back from the game.
- The playback bar stays hidden while the console has your mouse and keyboard.

### How to set it up

1. Connect your console: PS5 streaming, Xbox streaming, or your capture card.
2. Capture card: pair your PS5 once under *PS5 streaming*, then turn on *Control the PS5 through Remote Play*
   in the capture card window (or press the button under *Mouse and keyboard*).
3. Turn on *Mouse and keyboard* in quick setup and pick a layout under *Key bindings*.
4. Click the picture or press **F12** to start. F12 again or Alt+Tab gives your mouse and keyboard back.
