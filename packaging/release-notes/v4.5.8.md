## What's new in 4.5.8

### Discord status

- **Show your friends what you're playing through PushFPS.** Your Discord profile shows the game, the console
  and the frame rate, for example *GTA 6 · PS5 · 30 → 120 FPS*, with how long you've been playing and the
  PushFPS logo.
- Turn it on under *Settings → Recording → Instant replay and sharing → Discord status*. It's off until you
  switch it on.
- It only shows while a console's picture plays: a capture card, PS5 streaming, Xbox streaming or PC
  streaming. Videos, windows and files never show.
- The game's name comes from the game profile you picked in quick setup. *Show the game's name* leaves it
  out, so only the console and the frame rate show.
- It needs the Discord app running on the same PC; the browser version can't show it. If your friends don't
  see it, turn on activity sharing in Discord's settings under *Activity Privacy*.
- PushFPS only talks to the Discord app on your PC, and reads nothing about your Discord account.

### Fixes

- Automatic HUD detection keeps every box it finds.
- *Test my graphics card* lists every result.
- Streaming mode lists the sizes a camera offers.

### Capture cards

- The UGREEN 15389 (CM629) and the Benfei Video/Audio Capture Card are now listed as reported working by
  users.

### Linux and macOS

- HUD protection boxes, with automatic HUD detection, and game presets with share codes.
