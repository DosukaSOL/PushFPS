## What's new in 4.5.6

### Mouse and keyboard on a console

- **Play PS5 and Xbox games with your mouse and keyboard.** Works with PS5 Remote Play, Xbox streaming, and a
  capture card with *Control the PS5 through Remote Play* on. The console sees a controller: each key presses
  the button that does the same thing and the mouse turns the camera.
- **Your controller keeps working beside them.** Drive with the controller, get out of the car and aim with
  the mouse, without switching anything.
- **Layouts after Rockstar's PC keys**: GTA 6 (default and classic), GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. WASD to
  move, Shift to run, Space to jump, F / E to interact, Q for cover, Tab for weapons, the mouse to aim and
  shoot. Every key can be changed, with two keys per button.
- **Mouse settings**: sensitivity, vertical sensitivity, response curve, stick start and invert vertical.
- Click the picture or press **F12** to hand the mouse and keyboard to the console; F12 again or Alt+Tab takes
  them back. Function keys you haven't bound stay PushFPS shortcuts, so screenshots, recording and instant
  replay still work.
- Turn it on in quick setup (*Mouse and keyboard*) or in *Settings*.

### PS5 voice chat

- **Talk in party and game chat with your PC's microphone.** Turn on *PC microphone* in the PS5 window; it
  uses the microphone chosen in *Settings → Recording*. PushFPS tells you when the PS5 hears it.
- **F2** mutes and unmutes it, also while the console has the keyboard. The key can be changed in
  *Settings → Shortcuts*.

### Capture cards

- The Magewell Pro Capture HDMI 4K Plus LT is now listed as reported working by a user.
- The capture window no longer shows a Magewell low-latency switch and a Blackmagic native capture switch
  that PushFPS doesn't include. A Blackmagic card that had the native switch on shows up in the device list
  again.
