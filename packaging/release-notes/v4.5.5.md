## What's new in 4.5.5

### Virtual camera

- **Installing the camera component no longer freezes PushFPS.** While Windows asked for administrator
  permission, PushFPS could stop responding until it was closed. It now keeps running, and the permission
  window opens in front of PushFPS.
- The camera component is what lets programs that don't list *PushFPS Virtual Camera* on their own, such as
  some live streaming apps, see it.

### Capture cards

- The Monster Capture Card is now listed as reported working by a user.

### Fixes

- A display counter that Windows starts again no longer shows up in the log as a huge number.

### Linux and macOS

- A 90 FPS target on 90 Hz screens, the capture card row in quick setup, and crop and resize of the picture.
