## What's new in 2.1.9

**NVIDIA Image Scaling (NIS).** A new upscaling choice under *Professional, Quality, Super resolution*:
fast spatial upscaling with adaptive sharpening on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics, in SDR and HDR. Adjust
it with *NIS sharpening* (50 % is NVIDIA's default).

**Everything from Veyra 2.1.6** (by Likely7), merged into PushFPS:

- **Instant replay**: keeps the last minute (15 s to 10 min) and saves it with **F9**, also while you record.
- **Microphone recording**: mixed with the game sound or as its own audio track.
- **Exclude all effects** regions: keep the HUD or minimap untouched by effects and frame generation.
- Smoother capture: automatic duplicate-frame removal now follows changing console frame rates, the 60 to
  30 mode no longer catches up after a still picture, and frame delivery was reworked against stutter.
- Lower latency between the capture card and the screen, GPU priority High by default.
- HDR controls start folded; PS5 account lookup through PSNTools.

**Also new in PushFPS:** the saved-recording card now appears for instant replay clips too.

Not included: Veyra's experimental ANR frame generation (its developers do not allow it to be redistributed
yet). Every other PushFPS feature (quick setup, console and game profiles, picture and sound editor, sound
profiles, OBS plugin) is unchanged.
